# 🎧 Soundcore Space One Pro - Promotional Banner Design

An e-commerce promotional key visual (KV) designed for **Soundcore Space One Pro** headphones, focusing on high-converting marketing visuals, clear typography hierarchy, and strong brand aesthetics.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
* **Role:** Marketing Graphic Designer 
* **Target Audience:** Tech enthusiasts, audiophiles, and everyday music lovers looking for premium noise-canceling headphones.
* **Objective:** Create an eye-catching advertisement banner optimized for e-commerce platforms and social media campaigns to drive customer engagement and sales.

---

## 🎨 Key Design Elements & Highlights
* **Visual Hierarchy & Typography:** Standardized text sizes and contrast to ensure the tagline *"ESCAPE THE NOISE, FIND YOUR WORLD"* immediately captures attention.
* **Photo Manipulation & Composition:** Integrated glowing wave patterns and dynamic background graphics to evoke a sense of deep sound immersion and premium feel.
* **Feature Grid:** Structured product features (4-Stage Noise Cancelling, LDAC Hi-Res Audio, FlexiCurve™ Structure) into clean, icon-supported feature cards for quick readability.
* **Conversion-Driven Layout (CTA):** Embedded a prominent **SHOP NOW** button alongside multi-channel seller logos (Facebook, Shopee, Lazada) to streamline user conversion.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Skills Used
* **Tools Used:** Canva (Graphic & Banner Design)
* **Design Skills:** Visual Hierarchy, Layout & Composition, Color Theory, E-commerce Marketing Design, Branding & Key Visual Creation
